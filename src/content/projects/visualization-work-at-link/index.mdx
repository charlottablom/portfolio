## Context

During my time as a Junior Architect at LINK Arkitektur in Stockholm, I produced 3D visualizations and AI-assisted renderings across a wide range of client projects and tenders - developing vision images, editing existing renders, and testing furniture and material options, often working from other colleagues' base models, moodboards, or sketches.

### Sävar, for Umeå Municipality

For a new detaljplan outside Umeå, where the municipality is developing a commercial and industrial area alongside the Nordostlänken rail expansion, I modeled and rendered a full aerial vision image.

### Sigtuna, a birthday gift for King Carl XVI Gustaf

For King Carl XVI Gustaf's 80th birthday, Sigtunaskolan Humanistiska Läroverk - his former school - commissioned a new courtyard as a placemaking gift. **Ulrika Larsson**, a landscape architect at LINK, sketched the design by hand; I then tested it with different specified furniture using AI image editing, and the final image, with green benches, was presented as part of the gift itself.

### Husby, later reused for a Telge tender

For a housing project on Husby's Nordic-city-named streets - Molde and Oslo among them - I edited and refined renders from a base model by **Ellinor Sundström Starbring**, adding people and context. The images were later reused as reference material in a tender for Telge.
