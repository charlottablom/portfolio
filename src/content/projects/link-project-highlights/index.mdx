## Context

During my time as a Junior Architect at LINK Arkitektur in Stockholm, I produced 3D visualizations and AI-assisted renderings across a wide range of client projects and tenders. These three pieces span from large-scale site planning to a hand-sketched gift for a king.

### Sävar, for a municipality in the north of Sweden

For a new detaljplan, where the municipality is developing a commercial and industrial area alongside a rail expansion, I modeled and rendered a full aerial vision image.

### Sigtuna, a birthday gift for King Carl XVI Gustaf

For King Carl XVI Gustaf's 80th birthday, Sigtunaskolan Humanistiska Läroverk - his former school - commissioned a new courtyard as a placemaking gift, developed together with landscape architects at LINK. **Ulrika Larsson** sketched the design by hand; I then tested it with different specified furniture using AI image editing, and painted a set of watercolor renderings from the landscape architects' drawings, showing the courtyard within its wider school setting.
